How to Play
✕

🎯 Objective

Unscramble the letters to form valid words as fast as possible. Every correct answer scores points, and the highest scoring player or team wins.

🎮 Game Modes

FFA (Free For All): Four players compete individually. Each round, solve the word before the others to earn top points. Faster solves earn higher rewards.

Team Up: Two teams cooperate and compete. Each team member solves words to add to the shared team score. Work together to outscore the opposing team.

Ranked & Quick Matches: Play head-to-head or in small groups with faster timers and higher-stakes scoring.

⭐ Scoring

Correct words: Points are awarded for each valid answer.

Fastest answer: Highest points for the quickest correct submission.

Team score (Team Up): All correct answers count toward the team total.

Streak bonuses: Keep solving correctly to earn extra score boosts.

💡 Tips

• Type your answer and press Enter or click Submit.

• In FFA, prioritize speed and accuracy — the fastest solver wins the biggest rewards.

• In Team Up, communicate with your teammate and focus on building the shared team score.

• Use streaks to amplify your score and climb the leaderboard.

🏆 Ranking System

MMR increases with wins and decreases with losses in ranked matches. Higher MMR unlocks tougher words and better league placement.
